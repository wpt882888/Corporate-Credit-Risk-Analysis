# Corporate Credit Risk Analysis

### Case Study: Super Micro Computer, Inc. (NASDAQ: SMCI)

## Project Overview

This project examines the financial condition and credit risk profile of Super Micro Computer, Inc. using publicly available financial statements, SEC filings and other regulatory disclosures.

The analysis covers FY2024–FY2026, with a focus on financial performance, cash flow, working capital, corporate governance and compliance-related risks.

## Financial Analysis

Three years of financial data were collected and analysed using Excel. Key indicators include:

- R&D expense ratio
- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
- Operating cash flow and net income
- Liabilities-to-assets ratio
- Interest coverage ratio

An Excel-based credit risk scorecard was developed to assess the company's financial position and debt-servicing capacity.

## KYC & Due Diligence

The review covers auditor resignation, internal control weaknesses, ownership structure, related-party transactions, litigation and export-control-related disclosures.

Selected company and individual names were also checked against public OFAC and U.S. Consolidated Screening List databases. The screening was preliminary and does not constitute full KYC verification or sanctions clearance.

## Key Findings

- Financial Credit Risk Score: **3.00 / 5.00 – Moderate Risk**
- KYC & Compliance Risk Score: **3.55 / 5.00 – High Risk**

The analysis identified significant operating cash flow pressure, inventory growth, declining interest coverage and outstanding internal control concerns.
